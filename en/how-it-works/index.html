Where your money goes

  1. 1. Recipients are vetted

    Only vetted legal entities in Japan (social welfare corporations, NPOs and similar) receive gifts on this site. We do not handle donations to individuals.

  2. 2. Secure payment with Stripe

    Card details are processed by Stripe and never stored on this site.

  3. 3. Paid directly to the recipient

    Donations are paid via Stripe Connect straight into the recipient's own account. The operator never holds donated funds.

  4. 4. Impact reports

    Recipients report how the money was used, and we publish those reports here.

  5. Local governments, relief funds and overseas causes

    We link you to their official donation pages (furusato nozei, the Japanese Red Cross, GlobalGiving and others). The money does not pass through this site.

Fees and tax

  • Administrative fee: Lumière Conseil keeps 10% of each payment (5% for disaster emergency appeals). Card processing fees are paid by Lumière Conseil out of this. Any change will be announced on this page in advance.
  • Covering the fee: if you tick "add the administrative fee" on the donation form, the fee is added on top so the recipient receives the full amount you chose (on by default; you can untick it).
  • Tax deductions: depend on the recipient. Receipts are issued by the recipient.
