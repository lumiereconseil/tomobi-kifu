🌏 Give globally: help Japan from abroad, and the world from Japan

Live outside Japan and want to help Kumamoto recover? Or want to support disaster areas around the world? Here are verified ways to give, including options that accept international cards.

Covers
Japan's disaster areas and disaster-hit countries
Ways to give
21

If you live outside Japan, the appeals Japanese NGOs run on GlobalGiving (a US giving platform) accept international cards for the Kumamoto earthquake. International bank transfers to Kumamoto Prefecture, Kumamoto City and the Japanese Red Cross closed on 30–31 October 2026.

From Japan, gifts in yen to Japanese certified NPOs may qualify for a tax deduction. To reach small local groups, you can also give to GlobalGiving's funds. We only link to official pages and never hold your money.
