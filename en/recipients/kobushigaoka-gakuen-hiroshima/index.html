Kobushigaoka Gakuen Children's Home (Fukuyama, Hiroshima)

Supports children aged 2 to 18 at a children's home in Fukuyama.

A children's home run by the Kobushi no Mura welfare corporation in Kamo-cho, Fukuyama, Hiroshima, where children aged roughly 2 to 18 live in five family-style units. After review by the board, donations go to the running of the facilities that need them.
