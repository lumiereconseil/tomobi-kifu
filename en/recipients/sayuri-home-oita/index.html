Sayuri Home Children's Home (Oita City, Oita)

Improves the surroundings and independence of children at a home in Oita City.

A children's home in Oita City run by the Sayuri Aijien welfare corporation. Donors can choose the use, either care for the children living there or the corporation's running costs, and gifts help improve living conditions, individual support and aftercare.
