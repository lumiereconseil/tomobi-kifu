🏠 Children's Homes across Japan: where to donate

Children's homes across Japan caring for children who cannot live with their parents. We list only homes whose own official pages invite donations.

Area
Across Japan
Ways to give
40

Children's homes care for children who cannot live with their families because of abuse or other circumstances; most are run by social welfare corporations. Gifts fund daily life and activities, education and independence costs, and support for young people who have left care.

How to give differs by home: some take online payments, others bank or postal transfers, and some ask you to contact them first. Please check each home's page.

→ Relief funds, support funds and furusato nozei: our giving guide

Every organisation listed here is currently a link to its own official donation page. We are not affiliated with them, never hold the money and take no fee.
