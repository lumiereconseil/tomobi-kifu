🌅 Noto Peninsula Earthquake Recovery: how to donate

Municipalities and local organisations supporting Noto's recovery from the 2024 earthquake and the Oku-Noto heavy rain.

Area
Noto, Ishikawa Prefecture (Suzu, Nanao, Wajima and more)
Since
1 January 2024
Ways to give
4

Noto is still rebuilding after the earthquake of 1 January 2024 and the Oku-Noto heavy rain later that year. You can give to municipal relief funds or to organisations working locally.

→ Relief funds, support funds and furusato nozei: our giving guide

Every organisation listed here is currently a link to its own official donation page. We are not affiliated with them, never hold the money and take no fee.
