🏯 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake: how to donate

Official ways to give after the 2026 Kumamoto earthquake: relief funds for residents, gifts to municipalities, and support for relief organisations.

Area
Kumamoto Prefecture (Kumamoto, Yatsushiro, Hikawa, Uki and more)
Since
28 July 2026
Ways to give
14

Seismic intensity 7 was recorded in Hikawa and Uki. The prefecture, affected towns and cities, the Japanese Red Cross, the Community Chest and relief organisations are each accepting gifts.

Choose a relief fund to reach affected residents directly, a municipal donation to fund recovery projects, or a relief organisation to support work on the ground now. Our giving guide explains the difference.

→ Relief funds, support funds and furusato nozei: our giving guide

Every organisation listed here is currently a link to its own official donation page. We are not affiliated with them, never hold the money and take no fee.
