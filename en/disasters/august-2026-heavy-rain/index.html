⛈️ August 2026 Heavy Rain and Typhoon No. 25 (Kanagawa, Ishikawa, Fukui): how to donate

Official donation pages for Kanagawa, Ishikawa and Fukui after the August 2026 heavy rain and Typhoon No. 25. Chiba has its own page.

Area
Kanagawa, Ishikawa (Hakui and more) and Fukui prefectures
Since
27 August 2026
Ways to give
4

The August heavy rain hit Ishikawa and Fukui, and Typhoon No. 25 hit Kanagawa. The prefecture and community chests collect relief funds, and Hakui accepts disaster-support donations through furusato nozei sites.

Relief funds have deadlines: Kanagawa until 31 March 2027, the Ishikawa Community Chest until 29 January 2027, and the Fukui Community Chest until 30 November 2026.

→ Relief funds, support funds and furusato nozei: our giving guide

Every organisation listed here is currently a link to its own official donation page. We are not affiliated with them, never hold the money and take no fee.
