🏔️ 2026 Nepal Floods and Debris Flows: how to donate

Japanese organisations supporting people affected by the floods and debris flows in Nepal in August 2026.

Area
Central and northern Nepal
Since
26 August 2026
Ways to give
3

A large debris flow struck central Nepal on 26 August 2026, and floods and debris flows also hit the north. You can give from Japan through Japan Platform, CWS Japan and the Japanese Red Cross.

The Japanese Red Cross appeal is open until 30 November 2026.
