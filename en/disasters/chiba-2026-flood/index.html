☔ Chiba Floods: August 2026 Heavy Rain and Typhoon No. 25: how to donate

Official donation pages of Chiba Prefecture and Chiba City for people hit by the August 2026 heavy rain and Typhoon No. 25.

Area
Chiba Prefecture (Chiba City and more)
Since
13 August 2026
Ways to give
4

Chiba Prefecture runs one combined relief fund for the August heavy rain and September's Typhoon No. 25. A distribution committee allocates the money, which reaches affected households through their municipalities. It is open until 31 March 2027, by bank transfer.

The Japanese Red Cross also collects relief money for the Chiba heavy rain until 31 March 2027, all of it sent to Chiba Prefecture's distribution committee.

Chiba City runs both a relief fund paid directly to its affected residents (until 30 November 2026) and disaster-support donations for recovery through furusato nozei sites (no return gifts).
