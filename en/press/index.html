Press

Give Together is a free donation portal that gathers only official donation pages for the areas hit by the 2026 Kumamoto earthquake and the Chiba floods, and for children's homes and welfare groups across Japan. We welcome interviews and coverage.

Name
Give Together (ともに寄付)
Launched
October 2026
Operator
Lumière Conseil (sole proprietorship, Japan)
Listings
107 (98 in Japan, 9 overseas), including 14 for the 2026 Kumamoto earthquake, 4 for the Chiba floods and 40 children's homes
Languages
Japanese and English
Cost
Free for donors and listed organisations. Gifts go straight to each organisation's official channel
  • Only official donation pages of local governments and registered legal entities (social welfare corporations, certified NPOs and similar) are listed. No donations to individuals.
  • Every listing is checked on the organisation's own official page before it goes up.
  • A giving guide explains relief funds, support funds and furusato nozei so first-time donors can choose.
  • The site never holds donated money.
